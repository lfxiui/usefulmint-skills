# Visual system

Use a warm near-white #f7f7ed ground and one dark-green #547143 accent. One question, one main chart and a short takeaway per card. Source, denominator, unit and date are part of the final artifact, not hidden metadata. A ring needs circumference-based segments. Bar lengths must share one scale. The local sample has two separate synthetic questions: 144 of 200 respondents willing to record ideas, and inspiration-channel shares 35/25/25/15. It is not a real survey.
