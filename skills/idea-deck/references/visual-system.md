# Visual system

Use a 1200 × 750 layout, lavender #e9e0f5, plum #392952 and pale green #c4d796. Keep one title scale and consistent margins throughout. Use a sculptural vector form for the cover, three diagrammed steps for the approach and one clear directional visual on the closing page. Alternate light and dark pages deliberately. Keep headlines away from the primary form and preserve negative space.
