# Visual system

Default 1200 by 750 canvas, orange #de542a and warm white #fff4df. Photo occupies about half the composition. Headline normally 90–140px and no more than three short lines. Keep text clear of the product silhouette; inspect contrast and crop at final size.

Check the final artifact at its intended size: main subject visible, labels legible, hierarchy clear, no accidental clipping, keyboard actions reachable, and source attribution accurate. Reuse these principles for new content without copying a third-party layout.
