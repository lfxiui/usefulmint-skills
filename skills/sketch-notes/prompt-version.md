# Pasteable prompt

Turn source notes into an original illustrated knowledge card with editable SVG diagrams.

Identify 3–5 ideas and the relationships between them. Draw a clear diagram instead of placing paragraphs in boxes. Use a distinct visual symbol for each idea and visible directional arrows. Use short labels under each figure. When an idea cannot be illustrated, preserve its exact meaning rather than inventing evidence.

Default 1200 by 750 canvas, 56px safe margins, ink #212b25 on warm yellow #f5d945. Use 4–5px rounded linework, asymmetric but aligned diagram elements, headings 60–70px, labels at least 28px. Avoid tiny footnotes and unsupported facts.

Use my language and real material. Deliver SVG knowledge card with editable source, inspect the actual result, and report any unverified export. Do not publish or buy anything.
